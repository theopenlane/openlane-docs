# Writing docs in this repo

Every page under `docs/docs` follows the
[docs style guide](docs/docs/developers/contributing/docs-style-guide.mdx),
which applies the Openlane brand style guide to documentation. Read it before
writing or editing a page, and work through its pre-publish checklist before
finishing.

## Before finishing

Check the lines you added for contractions, em dashes, `- **Label**: text`
bullets, Title Case headings, ordered lists numbered anything but `1.`, and
the words in the prose habits table. Confirm that nothing coming soon is
described as live and that every opener confirms the reader is on the right
page instead of pitching the feature.
