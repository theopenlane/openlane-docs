# Settings

Your organization is your workspace in Openlane: your team, your data, your configuration. The pages here mirror `Organization Settings` and `User Management` in the console.

## Organization administration

[Organizations](./organizations/overview.mdx) covers what an organization is, your profile, and billing. [Custom Data](./custom-data.mdx) covers the organization-defined enums and tags that shape how your compliance data is classified.

## Access

[Authentication](./authentication/overview.mdx) describes the supported sign-in methods (email/password, social login, passkeys, single sign-on (SSO)) and when to use each, including [Single Sign-On](./authentication/sso.mdx) and [programmatic access](./authentication/programmatic.mdx).

## User management

[Members and Roles](./user-management/members.mdx) covers inviting people, roles, and how people join automatically. [Groups](./user-management/groups/overview.mdx) organize your people and carry permissions across objects, including the built-in Viewers, Admins, and All Members groups. [Permissions](./user-management/permissions.mdx) lays out role-based permissions, inheritance, and granular access controls across all object types.
