# Settings

Your organization is your workspace in Openlane: your team, your data, your configuration. The pages here mirror what you'll find under `Organization Settings` in the console.

## Organization Administration

- [Organizations](./organizations/overview.mdx): what an organization is, your profile, members and roles, and billing
- [Custom Data](./custom-data.mdx): organization-defined enums and tags that shape how your compliance data is classified

## Access

- [Authentication](./authentication/overview.mdx): the supported sign-in methods (email/password, social login, passkeys, single sign-on (SSO)) and when to use each, including [Single Sign-On](./authentication/sso.mdx) and [programmatic access](./authentication/programmatic.mdx)
- [Permissions](./authorization/overview.mdx): role-based permissions, inheritance, and granular access controls across all object types
- [Groups](./groups/overview.mdx): organize your people and carry permissions across objects, including the built-in Viewers, Admins, and All Members groups
