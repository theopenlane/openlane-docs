# Members and Roles

People become members of your organization through an invitation, by auto-joining on a verified email domain, or through your identity provider when SSO is enforced. Every member carries a role that determines what they can do. Member management lives under `Organization Settings` → `Members`.

## Inviting Members

<Steps>

1. Click **Invite Member**

2. Enter the person's email address and select their role (see [Roles](#roles) below)

3. Optionally assign them to specific groups

4. Click **Send Invitation**

</Steps>

The person receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. The link is valid for 14 days; if they don't accept in time, you can resend the invitation or create a new one. Invitations work regardless of your SSO settings, and an invitation marked SSO-exempt carries that exemption onto the resulting member.

:::tip
There are no per-user fees, so we recommend inviting your whole team rather than a single "compliance owner". The program works best when it reflects how your organization actually operates.
:::

## Roles

Each member has one role:

| Role | What it can do |
| ---- | -------------- |
| Owner | Full control, including billing, ownership transfer, and deleting the organization; always exempt from SSO enforcement so you cannot lock yourself out |
| Super Admin | Everything an admin can do, plus billing and subscriptions |
| Admin | Manages organization settings, members, and most objects; no billing access |
| Member | Day-to-day work with organization resources, including sending invites |
| Auditor | Read-oriented access designed for external auditors, with the ability to work with evidence and reviews |

The exact object-by-object permissions for each role are laid out in the [authorization matrix](../authorization/overview.mdx); when role-level access is too coarse, [groups](../groups/overview.mdx) refine it per object.

## Managing Members

The Members section lists every current member, their role, and when they joined.

- **Change a role**: find the member, click their current role, select the new role, and confirm.
- **Remove a member**: find the member, click the remove option, and confirm. Members can also leave the organization themselves at any time.

## Joining Automatically

Beyond invitations, there are two mechanisms that add people to your organization without a manual invite, and which one applies depends on whether SSO is enforced:

- **Auto-join by email domain** (SSO not enforced): anyone who verifies an email address on one of your allowed email domains is added as a member.
- **Just-in-time provisioning** (SSO enforced): anyone who successfully authenticates against your identity provider is added as a member, optionally restricted to specific email domains.

Both are configured under `Organization Settings` → `Authentication`, and the [SSO guide](../authentication/sso.mdx#getting-people-into-your-organization) covers the mechanics, the settings, and how to choose between them. Restricting allowed email domains also constrains who can be invited; free email providers like Gmail cannot be used as allowed domains.

:::tip
If you're working with a fractional CISO or consultant, adding their domain upfront (as an allowed domain, or as an [SSO exempt domain](../authentication/sso.mdx#exemptions) when SSO is enforced) makes collaboration much smoother.
:::
