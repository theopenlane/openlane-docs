# Billing and Subscriptions

Billing covers your payment details, subscription status, and the feature modules enabled for your organization, all under `Organization Settings` → `Billing`. Only Owners and Super Admins have billing access.

## Billing Contact

Keep your billing information up to date so we can reach you about payments and invoices:

- Billing contact name and email address
- Phone number (in international format, e.g., +12025551234)
- Billing address
- Tax ID if applicable, such as your VAT number or business registration number

## Payment Method

Click **Add Payment Method** in the Billing section and enter your payment details. We process payment information securely through Stripe; it is never stored directly in Openlane.

## Subscription

The Subscription section shows where your account stands:

- Your current plan and status
- Trial expiration date (if applicable)
- Next payment due date
- Subscription expiration (if you've cancelled)

## Feature Modules

We offer additional feature modules that add capabilities to your organization. Some are included in your plan; others carry an additional subscription fee.

<Steps>

1. Go to `Organization Settings` → `Billing` → `Subscriptions`
2. Browse the available feature modules
3. Click **Enable** on a module, review pricing, and confirm

</Steps>

The **Active Modules** section shows the status of each module (active, trial, inactive) and is where you manage module subscriptions.
