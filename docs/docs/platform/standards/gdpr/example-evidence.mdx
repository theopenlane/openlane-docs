# GDPR Example Evidence

The table below lists commonly evidenced control areas for GDPR and the artifacts that typically satisfy them; attach these to your controls as [evidence](/docs/platform/compliance-management/evidence/overview).

| Control Area | Example Evidence |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Quarterly vulnerability scans | Scan results covering each of the last four quarters |
| Annual penetration testing | Report from the most recent annual penetration test |
| Privacy policy inclusions | The published privacy notice from your website showing the required disclosures |
| Privacy policy acceptance | Screenshots of the registration flow demonstrating that users must explicitly agree to the privacy notice before sign-up completes |
| Minimal information required | A completed record of processing activities (ROPA) documenting how each collected PII field will be used |
| Annual review of processing purposes | Minutes from management's yearly review of privacy policies |
| Purposeful use only | The section of your privacy notice or policy stating that personal data is used only for its declared purposes |
| Tracking breaches of PII | Screenshots from the tracking system used to log breaches and security incidents involving personal data |
| Notice of breach to affected users | Documented breach notification procedures and the template used to notify affected individuals |
| Business continuity plan | Current business continuity plan |
| Vulnerability management | Current vulnerability management policy |
| Scope of privacy program | A document summarizing the scope of your privacy program, drawing on your ROPA |
| Designated data protection official | Documentation formally appointing a data protection officer (DPO) |
| Management of sensitive information | Data classification policy covering classification and handling of PII, plus security awareness materials that address PII handling and how to report issues |
| Data subject consent | Documentation of how consent is captured before processing; screenshots of consent mechanisms such as a checkbox on a registration form; records of consent kept in systems such as your CRM |
| Withdrawal of consent | Privacy notice language explaining how consent can be withdrawn; screenshots of opt-out mechanisms such as unsubscribe links in email footers; records of withdrawals and the follow-up actions taken |
| Notification of disclosures to third parties | Process documentation or templates for notifying stakeholders when PII is disclosed to third parties, plus records of those disclosures |
| International transfer of personal data | Privacy notice and data processing agreement (DPA) language describing how cross-border transfers are handled |
| Joint controllers | Privacy notice and DPA language defining each controller's roles and responsibilities where joint controllers are used |
| Communication of obligations to data subjects | Privacy notice describing your privacy obligations and the channels for making requests; records of data subject requests (DSRs) and their resolution; any response templates used |
| Organizational context | Documentation describing where your organization sits in the data processing ecosystem, for example as controller, processor, or subprocessor |
| Record of processing activities (ROPA) | Completed ROPA covering each population whose data you process, such as customers, prospects, website visitors, and employees |
| Data processing agreements | The DPA template used when sharing PII with third parties, and fully executed contracts that incorporate it |
| Data protection impact assessment (DPIA) | Completed DPIA documentation, prepared before processing begins; separate assessments per use case can help when processing is complex |
| Collection of special category PII | ROPA entries identifying whether special categories of PII are collected and which allowable conditions are relied on |
| Tracking and response to data subject requests | A log of DSRs received and the actions taken to resolve each one |
| Management of data subject rights | Privacy notice explaining how data subjects exercise their GDPR rights, such as a dedicated inbox or web form; the DSR log; internal runbooks used to fulfill requests such as deletion |
| Representative for non-EU controllers/processors | Documentation designating an EU representative, where one is required for your organization |
