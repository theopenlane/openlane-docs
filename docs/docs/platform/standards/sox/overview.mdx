# SOX Compliance Overview

The Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) ensures that financial reporting by publicly traded companies in the U.S. is accurate, complete, and transparent, protecting investors and the public from accounting fraud.

  fullName={"Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002"}

  managedBy={"SEC; PCAOB oversees public company auditors"}
  appliesTo={"US publicly traded companies and their external auditors"}
  certification={"No: annual independent audit of internal controls over financial reporting (Section 404)"}

## What does SOX compliance involve?

* <Term t="internal-control">Internal controls</Term>: Establishing and maintaining controls for financial records
* Data security: Safeguarding financial data, tracking breaches, and logging electronic records
* External audits: Having an independent audit firm review financial statements
* Accountability: Ensuring senior executives are responsible for the accuracy of financial reports
* Corporate governance: Adhering to administrative policies and ensuring proper governance
