# ISO 42001 Example Evidence

The table below lists commonly evidenced control areas for ISO 42001 and the artifacts that typically satisfy them; attach these to your controls as [evidence](/docs/platform/compliance-management/evidence/overview).

| Control Area | Example Evidence |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Artificial intelligence management system (AIMS) scope | A documented scope statement describing the boundaries of your AI management system |
| Statement of Applicability | The SoA listing each control, whether it applies, and its implementation status |
| Management system plan | The formal plan describing how the AIMS is established, operated, and maintained |
| Management system objectives | Measurable objectives with owners, timelines, and the KPIs used to track them |
| Management review | Agendas and minutes from management reviews, including inputs considered and decisions or actions taken |
| Measurement and monitoring plan | A plan defining what is measured, the methods used, measurement frequency, and thresholds |
| Records management and control | The records control procedure covering retention, access, and integrity, plus a records inventory |
| Management of nonconformities | The nonconformity procedure together with corrective action records and root cause analyses |
| Periodic policy reviews | A policy review schedule and version history showing that reviews occur on the defined cadence |
| Continuous control monitoring | Exports or screenshots of your control library and monitoring dashboards showing control test status |
| Legal and regulatory requirements | A legal and compliance register, the policies that address each obligation, and meeting minutes from reviews |
| AI governance policy | The approved policy covering roles, oversight, and accountability for AI |
| AI committees | The committee charter, membership list, meeting cadence, and recent minutes |
| Organizational chart | An org chart diagram or an export from your HR system |
| Defined roles and responsibilities | Role descriptions, RACI matrices, or a responsibility matrix covering AI and security duties |
| AI resources | A resourcing plan or budget, a tooling inventory, and staffing or role assignments for AI work |
| AI training | Training materials and completion records for personnel in AI-relevant roles |
| AI practitioner proficiency | A role-based competency matrix with qualifications, certifications, and training completions |
| Risk assessment policy | The documented risk assessment policy |
| Periodic risk assessment | The most recently completed risk assessment report |
| Risk treatment plan | Risk register entries mapped to treatment decisions, with assigned owners and target dates |
| Risk tolerance | Risk appetite and tolerance statements, and the decision criteria approved by leadership |
| Residual risk | The risk register with documented residual risk assessments and formal risk acceptance approvals |
| AI risk management policy | The policy defining how AI risks are identified, assessed, treated, and monitored |
| AI risk tracking | The AI risk register and ongoing monitoring reports showing risk status, owners, and actions |
| AI impact assessment | A completed impact assessment covering intended use, affected stakeholders, and potential harms and benefits |
| AI development and evaluation policy | The formal policy governing how AI systems are developed and evaluated |
| Human oversight over AI systems | Documented oversight procedures and records from your model or use case approval workflow |
| AI fairness and bias evaluation | Fairness and bias testing reports, dataset analysis, evaluation metrics, and remediation actions taken |
| AI data management | Data management plans and procedures, dataset documentation, and records showing they are followed |
| AI feedback management | The documented process for collecting, triaging, and responding to internal and external feedback about AI systems |
| AI model environmental impact | An environmental impact assessment or sustainability reports from your cloud providers |
| User and system guides | End-user guides, administrator and operations guides, and runbooks for AI systems |
| Expectations of interested parties | A stakeholder register listing needs and expectations, mapped to the controls that address them |
| Architectural diagram | Current architecture diagrams, with confirmation from responsible personnel that they are accurate |
| Baseline configurations | Hardening standards such as Center for Internet Security (CIS) benchmarks or vendor guidance, and evidence they are applied |
| Change review process | Records showing peer review, approval, and enforced segregation of duties for production deployments |
| Testing of changes | Tickets for a sample of changes showing that testing occurred before release |
| Change releases approved | Pull requests or release records demonstrating authorization prior to deployment |
| Policies and procedures for logging | The logging and monitoring policy |
| Audit log contents | A sample log export showing the attributes captured, such as actor, action, and timestamp |
| Security events tracked and evaluated | Monitoring dashboards, alert queues, and incident tickets with triage notes |
| On-call team | On-call rotation schedules, escalation policies, and handoff notes |
| Incident response plan | The formal incident response plan |
| Incident response team | Documentation describing team roles, responsibilities, and escalation procedures |
| Incident response test | Records of tabletop or live exercises, including results, lessons learned, and participants |
| Procedures for unauthorized personally identifiable information (PII) disclosure | Procedures describing detection, containment, reporting, and remediation steps |
| Documentation of breaches involving PII | Screenshots or exports from the tracking system used to log breaches and unauthorized disclosures |
| Notification of incidents or breaches | Notification templates, communication logs, and the incident timeline |
| Communications plan | The formal communications plan |
| Internal communication channels | Screenshots from messaging or paging tools showing how security and AI events are communicated |
| External communication channels | Screenshots of customer support portals or help desk pages |
| Vendor management policy | The documented vendor management policy |
