# C1.1

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"C1.1"}

  description={"The entity identifies and maintains confidential information to meet the entity’s objectives related to confidentiality."}

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Defines and Identifies Confidential information</summary>

Procedures are in place to define, identify and designate confidential information when it is received or created.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Retains Confidential Information</summary>

Confidential information is retained for no longer than necessary to fulfill the identified purpose, unless a law or regulation specifically requires otherwise.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Protects Confidential Information from Destruction</summary>

Policies and procedures are in place to protect confidential information from erasure or destruction during the specified retention period of the information.

</details>
