## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Identifies Confidential Information for Destruction</summary>

Procedures are in place to identify confidential information requiring destruction when the end of the retention period is reached.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Destroys Confidential Information</summary>

Policies and procedures are in place to automatically or manually erase or otherwise destroy confidential information that has been identified for destruction.

</details>
