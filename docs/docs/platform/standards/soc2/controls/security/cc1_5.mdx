## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Enforces Accountability Through Structures, Authorities, and Responsibilities</summary>

Management and the board of directors establish the mechanisms to communicate and hold individuals accountable for performance of internal control responsibilities across the entity and implement corrective action as necessary.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Establishes Performance Measures, Incentives, and Rewards</summary>

Management and the board of directors establish performance measures, incentives, and other rewards appropriate for responsibilities at all levels of the entity, reflecting appropriate dimensions of performance and expected standards of conduct, and considering the achievement of both short-term and longer-term objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates Performance Measures, Incentives, and Rewards for Ongoing Relevance</summary>

Management and the board of directors align incentives and rewards with the fulfillment of internal control responsibilities in the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Excessive Pressures</summary>

Management and the board of directors evaluate and adjust pressures associated with the achievement of objectives as they assign responsibilities, develop performance measures, and evaluate performance.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates Performance and Rewards or Disciplines Individuals</summary>

Management and the board of directors evaluate performance of internal control responsibilities, including adherence to standards of conduct and expected levels of competence, and provide rewards or exercise disciplinary action, as appropriate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Takes Disciplinary Actions</summary>

A sanctions process is defined, and applied as needed, when an employee violates the entity's privacy policies or when an employee's negligent behavior causes a privacy incident.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC1.5.1** - Employee sanction policies are documented to communicate consequences for disciplinary actions, up to and including termination, for violations to company policies and the code of conduct.
- **CC1.5.2** - A whistleblower protection policy and ethics hotline is in place for employees to anonymously report violations, complaints or concerns related to company policies and the code of conduct.
- **CC1.5.3** - Management conducts a performance review of employees on an annual basis to evaluate individual performance against expected levels of performance and conduct.
- **CC1.5.4** - Employees are required to complete new hire security awareness training and annual security awareness training thereafter.
- **CC1.5.5** - Employees complete an acknowledgment form upon hire indicating that they have been given access to the employee manual and understand their responsibility for adhering to the code of conduct.
- **CC1.5.6** - New hires are required to complete an acknowledgment form upon hire consenting to not disclose confidential or proprietary information to unauthorized parties.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Control environment integrity** - The organization demonstrates commitment to integrity and ethical values through documented policies, procedures, and accountability measures.
