# CC9.1

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC9.1"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Risk Mitigation"}
  description={"The entity identifies, selects, and develops risk mitigation activities for risks arising from potential business disruptions."}

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Considers Mitigation of Risks of Business Disruption</summary>

Risk mitigation activities include the development of planned policies, procedures, communications, and alternative processing solutions to respond to, mitigate, and recover from incidents that disrupt business operations. Those resilience policies and procedures include monitoring processes, information, and communications to support the achievement of the entity's objectives during response, mitigation, and recovery efforts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Use of Insurance to Mitigate Financial Impact Risks</summary>

The risk management activities consider the use of insurance to offset the financial impact of loss events that would otherwise impair the ability of the entity to support the achievement of its objectives.

</details>

## Policies

- **[Risk Management Standard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theopenlane/policy-hub/main/risk-management/risk-management.md)** - Establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC9.1.1** - A business continuity plan is documented and outlines the range of disaster scenarios and steps that the business will take to ensure the resumption of critical business operations.
- **CC9.1.2** - Documented policies and procedures are in place to guide personnel in the identification, selection, and development of risk management activities for risks arising from potential business disruptions.
- **CC9.1.3** - A risk assessment is performed on an annual basis that considers risks arising from potential business disruptions. Identified risks are rated using a risk evaluation process and are formally documented with mitigation strategies.
- **CC9.1.5** - Risk mitigation activities include the use of insurance to offset the financial impact of loss events that would otherwise impair the ability of the entity to meet its objectives.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Business continuity preparedness** - Business continuity and disaster recovery plans are documented and tested to ensure service availability.

## Example Evidence

- **Annual Risk Assessment Report** - Annual risk assessment covering objectives, fraud, change, and business disruption risks with documented ratings and mitigation strategies
- **Business Continuity Plan** - Documented business continuity plan outlining disaster scenarios and steps to resume critical business operations
- **Cyber Liability Insurance Certificate** - Insurance certificate showing use of insurance to offset the financial impact of loss events
