# CC1.4

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC1.4"}
  name={"Demonstrates commitment to competence"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Control Environment"}
  description={"The entity demonstrates a commitment to attract, develop, and retain competent individuals in alignment with objectives. (COSO Principle 4)"}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Establishes Policies and Practices</summary>

Policies and practices reflect expectations of competence necessary to support the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates Competence and Addresses Shortcomings</summary>

The board of directors and management evaluate competence across the entity and in outsourced service providers in relation to established policies and practices and act as necessary to address shortcomings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Attracts, Develops, and Retains Individuals</summary>

The entity provides the mentoring and training needed to attract, develop, and retain sufficient and competent personnel and outsourced service providers to support the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Plans and Prepares for Succession</summary>

Senior management and the board of directors develop contingency plans for assignments of responsibility important for internal control.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Background of Individuals</summary>

The entity considers the background of potential and existing personnel, contractors, and vendor employees when determining whether to employ and retain the individuals.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Technical Competency of Individuals</summary>

The entity considers the technical competency of potential and existing personnel, contractors, and vendor employees when determining whether to employ and retain the individuals.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Provides Training to Maintain Technical Competencies</summary>

The entity provides training programs, including continuing education and training, to ensure skill sets and technical competency of existing personnel, contractors, and vendor employees are developed and maintained.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC1.4.1** - New employee hiring procedures are in place to guide the hiring process and include verification that candidates possess the required qualifications to perform the duties as outlined in the job description.
- **CC1.4.2** - Training courses are available to new and existing employees to maintain and advance the skill level of personnel.
- **CC1.4.3** - Employees are required to complete new hire security awareness training and annual security awareness training thereafter to understand their obligations and responsibilities to comply with security policies.
- **CC1.4.4** - Management conducts a performance review of employees on an annual basis to evaluate individual performance against expected levels of performance and conduct.

- **Control environment integrity** - The organization demonstrates commitment to integrity and ethical values through documented policies, procedures, and accountability measures.
