## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Communicates to External Parties</summary>

Processes are in place to communicate relevant and timely information to external parties, including shareholders, partners, owners, regulators, customers, financial analysts, and other external parties.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Enables Inbound Communications</summary>

Open communication channels allow input from customers, consumers, suppliers, external auditors, regulators, financial analysts, and others, providing management and the board of directors with relevant information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates With the Board of Directors</summary>

Relevant information resulting from assessments conducted by external parties is communicated to the board of directors.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Provides Separate Communication Lines</summary>

Separate communication channels, such as whistle-blower hotlines, are in place and serve as fail-safe mechanisms to enable anonymous or confidential communication when normal channels are inoperative or ineffective.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Selects Relevant Method of Communication</summary>

The method of communication considers the timing, audience, and nature of the communication and legal, regulatory, and fiduciary requirements and expectations.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Objectives Related to Confidentiality and Changes to Those Objectives</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria for CONFIDENTIALITY) The entity communicates, to external users, vendors, business partners, and others whose products andor services, or both, are part of the system, objectives and changes to the entity's objectives related to confidentiality and the protection of confidential information, as well as changes to those objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Objectives Related to Privacy and Changes to Those Objectives</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria for PRIVACY) The entity communicates, to external users, vendors, business partners, and others whose products andor services, or both, are part of the system, the entity's objectives related to privacy and the protection of personal information, as well as changes to those objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Incident Reporting Methods</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria for PRIVACY) The entity communicates to user entities, third parties, data subjects, and others the process used to report a suspected privacy incident.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Information About System Operation and Boundaries</summary>

(At system level) The entity prepares and communicates information about the design and operation of the system and its boundaries to authorized external users to permit users to understand their role in the system and the results of system operation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates System Objectives</summary>

(At system level) The entity communicates its system objectives to appropriate external users.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates System Responsibilities</summary>

(At system level) External users with responsibility for designing, developing, implementing, operating, maintaining, and monitoring system controls receive information about such responsibilities and have the information necessary to carry out such responsibilities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Information on Reporting System Failures, Incidents, Concerns, and Other Matters</summary>

(At system level) External users are provided with information on how to report systems failures, incidents, concerns, and other complaints to appropriate entity personnel.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC2.3.1** - Digital subscriber agreement and customer self-registration process includes required agreement acknowledgment.
- **CC2.3.2** - The customer portal is configured to allow customers to report problems, issues, and incidents.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Information and communication quality** - Relevant information is identified, captured, and communicated to internal and external parties to support the functioning of internal controls.
