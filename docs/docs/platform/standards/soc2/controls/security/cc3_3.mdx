## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Considers Various Types of Fraud</summary>

The assessment of fraud considers fraudulent reporting, possible loss of assets, and corruption resulting from the various ways that fraud and misconduct can occur.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Assesses Incentives and Pressures</summary>

The assessment of fraud risks considers incentives and pressures.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Assesses Opportunities</summary>

The assessment of fraud risk considers opportunities for unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposal of assets, altering the entity's reporting records, or committing other inappropriate acts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Assesses Attitudes and Rationalizations</summary>

The assessment of fraud risk considers how management and other personnel might engage in or justify inappropriate actions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Risks Related to the Use of IT and Access to Information</summary>

The assessment of fraud risks includes consideration of internal and external threats and vulnerabilities that arise specifically from the use of IT and access to information.

</details>
