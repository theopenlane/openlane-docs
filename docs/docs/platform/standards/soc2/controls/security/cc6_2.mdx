# CC6.2

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Creates Access Credentials to Protected Information Assets</summary>

The entity creates credentials for accessing protected information assets based on an authorization from the system's asset owner or authorized custodian. Authorization is required for the creation of all types of credentials of individuals (for example, employees, contractors, vendors, and business partner personnel), systems, and software.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reviews Validity of Access Credentials</summary>

The entity reviews access credentials on a periodic basis for validity (for example, employees, contractors, vendors, and business partner personnel) and inappropriate system or service accounts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Prevents the Use of Credentials When No Longer Valid</summary>

Processes are in place to disable, destroy, or otherwise prevent the use of access credentials when no longer valid.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC6.2.1** - Documented policies and procedures are in place regarding system settings, authentication, access, and security monitoring.
- **CC6.2.2** - Logical access to systems is approved and granted to an employee as a component of the hiring process.
- **CC6.2.3** - Logical access to systems is revoked as a component of the termination process.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Access control enforcement** - Access to systems and data is restricted to authorized individuals through proper provisioning and deprovisioning controls.

## Example Control Implementations

- **identity-provider-sso** - Access control is implemented through Google Workspace as the primary identity provider. All employee accounts are managed centrally with single sign-on (SSO) integration to business applications. Implementation includes: - Google Workspace serves as the identity provider for all employees - SSO is configured for GitHub, GCP Console, AWS Console, Slack, Notion, and other tools - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enforced organization-wide via Google Workspace security policies - User provisioning and deprovisioning follow documented onboarding/offboarding procedures
