## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Considers All Structures of the Entity</summary>

Management and the board of directors consider the multiple structures used (including operating units, legal entities, geographic distribution, and outsourced service providers) to support the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Establishes Reporting Lines</summary>

Management designs and evaluates lines of reporting for each entity structure to enable execution of authorities and responsibilities and flow of information to manage the activities of the entity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Defines, Assigns, and Limits Authorities and Responsibilities</summary>

Management and the board of directors delegate authority, define responsibilities, and use appropriate processes and technology to assign responsibility and segregate duties as necessary at the various levels of the organization.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Addresses Specific Requirements When Defining Authorities and Responsibilities</summary>

Management and the board of directors consider requirements relevant to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy when defining authorities and responsibilities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Interactions With External Parties When Establishing Structures, Reporting Lines, Authorities, and Responsibilities</summary>

Management and the board of directors consider the need for the entity to interact with and monitor the activities of external parties when establishing structures, reporting lines, authorities, and responsibilities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Establishes Structures, Reporting Lines, and Authorities to Support Compliance With Legal and Contractual Privacy Requirements</summary>

When establishing structures, reporting lines, and authorities, management considers legal and contractual privacy requirements and objectives.

</details>

## Policies

- **[Information Security Management Standard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theopenlane/policy-hub/main/information-security/information-security.md)** - Overarching policy defining the organization's approach to information security

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC1.3.1** - Organizational charts are in place to communicate the defined key areas of authority, responsibility, and lines of reporting to personnel.
- **CC1.3.2** - Documented position descriptions are in place to define the skills, responsibilities, and knowledge levels required for particular jobs.
- **CC1.3.3** - Senior management has assigned authorities for defining and implementing security policies to the designated security leadership.
- **CC1.3.4** - Leadership planning meetings are held on a quarterly basis to review audit plans and business operations.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Control environment integrity** - The organization demonstrates commitment to integrity and ethical values through documented policies, procedures, and accountability measures.

## Example Evidence

- **Information Security Roles and Responsibilities Matrix** - Documented assignment of security policy definition and implementation authorities to designated security leadership
- **Quarterly Leadership Meeting Minutes** - Minutes from quarterly leadership planning meetings covering audit plans and business operations review
- **Organizational Chart** - Current organizational chart showing defined areas of authority, responsibility, and reporting lines
- **Position Description Samples** - Documented position descriptions defining skills, responsibilities, and knowledge levels for key roles
