# CC5.1

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC5.1"}
  name={"Selects and develops control activities"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Control Activities"}
  description={"The entity selects and develops control activities that contribute to the mitigation of risks to the achievement of objectives to acceptable levels. (COSO Principle 10)"}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Integrates With Risk Assessment</summary>

Control activities help ensure that risk responses that address and mitigate risks are carried out.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Entity-Specific Factors</summary>

Management considers how the environment, complexity, nature, and scope of its operations, as well as the specific characteristics of its organization, affect the selection and development of control activities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Determines Relevant Business Processes</summary>

Management determines which relevant business processes require control activities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates a Mix of Control Activity Types</summary>

Control activities include a range and variety of controls and may include a balance of approaches to mitigate risks, considering both manual and automated controls, and preventive and detective controls.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers at What Level Activities Are Applied</summary>

Management considers control activities at various levels in the entity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Addresses Segregation of Duties</summary>

Management segregates incompatible duties, and where such segregation is not practical, management selects and develops alternative control activities.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC5.1.1** - System access is restricted to authorized individuals commensurate with their job responsibilities.
- **CC5.1.2** - Incidents are tracked, addressed, and resolved in a timely manner.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Control activity implementation** - Control activities are selected and developed to mitigate risks and achieve objectives.

## Example Evidence

- **Role-Based Access Control Matrix** - Access matrix showing system access is restricted to authorized individuals commensurate with job responsibilities using predefined security groups
- **Incident Tracking Register** - Incident register demonstrating incidents are tracked, addressed, and resolved in a timely manner
