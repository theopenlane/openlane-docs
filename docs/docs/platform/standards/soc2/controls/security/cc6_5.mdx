# CC6.5

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC6.5"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Logical and Physical Access Controls"}
  description={"The entity discontinues logical and physical protections over physical assets only after the ability to read or recover data and software from those assets has been diminished and is no longer required to meet the entity’s objectives."}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Removes Data and Software for Disposal</summary>

Procedures are in place to remove, delete, or otherwise render data and software inaccessible from physical assets and other devices owned by the entity, its vendors, and employees when the data and software are no longer required on the asset or the asset will no longer be under the control of the entity.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC6.5.1** - Data classification policies and procedures are documented and implemented.
- **CC6.5.2** - Data disposal and destruction policies and procedures are documented and implemented.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Data protection and classification** - Data is classified and protected from unauthorized disclosure throughout its lifecycle.

## Example Evidence

- **Data Classification Procedures** - Documented and implemented data classification policies and procedures per the Data Classification Policy
- **Data Disposal Records** - Records demonstrating data disposal and destruction procedures are documented and implemented
