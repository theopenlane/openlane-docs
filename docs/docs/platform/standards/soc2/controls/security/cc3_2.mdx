# CC3.2

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC3.2"}
  name={"Identifies and analyzes risk"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Risk Assessment"}
  description={"The entity identifies risks to the achievement of its objectives across the entity and analyzes risks as a basis for determining how the risks should be managed. (COSO Principle 7)"}

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Includes Entity, Subsidiary, Division, Operating Unit, and Functional Levels</summary>

The entity identifies and assesses risk at the entity, subsidiary, division, operating unit, and functional levels relevant to the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Analyzes Internal and External Factors</summary>

Risk identification considers both internal and external factors and their impact on the achievement of objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Involves Appropriate Levels of Management</summary>

The entity puts into place effective risk assessment mechanisms that involve appropriate levels of management.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Estimates Significance of Risks Identified</summary>

Identified risks are analyzed through a process that includes estimating the potential significance of the risk.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Determines How to Respond to Risks</summary>

Risk assessment includes considering how the risk should be managed and whether to accept, avoid, reduce, or share the risk.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Identifies Threats to Objectives</summary>

The entity identifies threats to the achievement of its objectives from intentional (including malicious) and unintentional acts and environmental events.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Identifies Vulnerability of System Components</summary>

The entity identifies the vulnerabilities of system components, including system processes, infrastructure, software, and other information assets.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Analyzes Threats and Vulnerabilities From Vendors, Business Partners, and Other Parties</summary>

The entity's risk assessment process includes the analysis of potential threats and vulnerabilities arising from vendors providing goods and services, as well as threats and vulnerabilities arising from business partners, customers, and other third parties with access to the entity's information systems.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Assesses the Significance of the Risks</summary>

The entity assesses the significance of the identified risks, including (1) determining the criticality of system components, including information assets, in achieving the objectives; (2) assessing the susceptibility of the identified vulnerabilities to the identified threats (3) assessing the likelihood of the identified risks (4) assessing the magnitude of the effect of potential risks to the achievement of the objectives; (5) considering the potential effects of unidentified threats and vulnerabilities on the assessed risks; (6) developing risk mitigation strategies to address the assessed risks; and (7) evaluating the appropriateness of residual risk (including whether to accept, reduce, or share such risks).

</details>

## Policies

- **[Risk Management Standard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theopenlane/policy-hub/main/risk-management/risk-management.md)** - Establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC3.2.1** - A risk assessment is performed on an annual basis that considers risks to achievement of objectives. Identified risks are rated using a risk evaluation process and formally documented with mitigation strategies.
- **CC3.2.2** - Fraud scoring and monitoring is in place for user accounts to detect and review suspicious activity.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Risk identification and management** - Risks to achievement of objectives are identified, analyzed, and managed through a formal risk assessment process.

## Example Evidence

- **Annual Risk Assessment Report** - Annual risk assessment covering objectives, fraud, change, and business disruption risks with documented ratings and mitigation strategies
- **Fraud Detection Monitoring Configuration** - Fraud scoring and monitoring configuration for user accounts showing detection and review of suspicious activity
