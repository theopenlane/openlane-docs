# CC7.4

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC7.4"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"System Operations"}
  description={"The entity responds to identified security incidents by executing a defined incident response program to understand, contain, remediate, and communicate security incidents, as appropriate."}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Assigns Roles and Responsibilities</summary>

Roles and responsibilities for the design, implementation, maintenance, and execution of the incident response program are assigned, including the use of external resources when necessary.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Contains and Responds to Security Incidents</summary>

Procedures are in place to respond to and contain security incidents that actively threaten entity objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Mitigates Ongoing Security Incidents</summary>

Procedures are in place to mitigate the effects of ongoing security incidents.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Resolves Security Incidents</summary>

Procedures are in place to resolve security incidents through closure of vulnerabilities, removal of unauthorized access, and other remediation actions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Restores Operations</summary>

Procedures are in place to restore data and business operations to an interim state that permits the achievement of entity objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Develops and Implements Communication of Security Incidents</summary>

Protocols for communicating, in a timely manner, information regarding security incidents and actions taken to affected parties are developed and implemented to support the achievement of the entity's objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Obtains Understanding of Nature of Incident and Determines Containment Strategy</summary>

An understanding of the nature (for example, the method by which the incident occurred and the affected system resources) and severity of the security incident is obtained to determine the appropriate response and containment strategy, including- (1) a determination of the appropriate response time frame, and (2) the determination and execution of the containment approach.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Remediates Identified Vulnerabilities</summary>

Identified vulnerabilities are remediated through the development and execution of remediation activities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Remediation Activities</summary>

Remediation activities are documented and communicated in accordance with the incident response program.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates the Effectiveness of Incident Response</summary>

The design of incident response activities is evaluated for effectiveness on a periodic basis.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Periodically Evaluates Incidents</summary>

Periodically, management reviews incidents related to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy and identifies the need for system changes based on incident patterns and root causes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Applies Breach Response Procedures</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria of PRIVACY) Breach response procedures are defined and applied in the event of a confirmed privacy incident.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates Unauthorized Use and Disclosure</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria of PRIVACY) Events that resulted in unauthorized use or disclosure of personal information are communicated to the data subjects, legal and regulatory authorities, and others as required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Application of Sanctions</summary>

(For Trust Service Criteria of PRIVACY) The conduct of individuals and organizations operating under the authority of the entity and involved in the unauthorized use or disclosure of personal information is evaluated and, if appropriate, sanctioned in accordance with entity policies and legal and regulatory requirements.

</details>
