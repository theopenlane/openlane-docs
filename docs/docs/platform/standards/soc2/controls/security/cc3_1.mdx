## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Reflects Management's Choices</summary>

Operations objectives reflect management's choices about structure, industry considerations, and performance of the entity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Tolerances for Risk</summary>

Management considers the acceptable levels of variation relative to the achievement of operations objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Includes Operations and Financial Performance Goals</summary>

The organization reflects the desired level of operations and financial performance for the entity within operations objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Forms a Basis for Committing of Resources</summary>

Management uses operations objectives as a basis for allocating resources needed to attain desired operations and financial performance. External Financial Reporting Objectives:

</details>

<details>
<summary>Complies With Applicable Accounting Standards</summary>

Financial reporting objectives are consistent with accounting principles suitable and available for that entity. The accounting principles selected are appropriate in the circumstances.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Materiality</summary>

Management considers materiality in financial statement presentation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflects Entity Activities</summary>

External reporting reflects the underlying transactions and events to show qualitative characteristics and assertions. External Nonfinancial Reporting Objectives:

</details>

<details>
<summary>Complies With Externally Established Frameworks</summary>

Management establishes objectives consistent with laws and regulations or standards and frameworks of recognized external organizations.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Required Level of Precision</summary>

Management reflects the required level of precision and accuracy suitable for user needs and based on criteria established by third parties in nonfinancial reporting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflects Entity Activities</summary>

External reporting reflects the underlying transactions and events within a range of acceptable limits. Internal Reporting Objectives:

</details>

<details>

Internal reporting provides management with accurate and complete information regarding management's choices and information needed in managing the entity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers the Required Level of Precision</summary>

Management reflects the required level of precision and accuracy suitable for user needs in nonfinancial reporting objectives and materiality within financial reporting objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflects Entity Activities</summary>

Internal reporting reflects the underlying transactions and events within a range of acceptable limits. Compliance Objectives:

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reflects External Laws and Regulations</summary>

Laws and regulations establish minimum standards of conduct, which the entity integrates into compliance objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Tolerances for Risk</summary>

Management considers the acceptable levels of variation relative to the achievement of operations objectives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Establishes Sub-Objectives for Risk Management</summary>

Management identifies sub-objectives for use in risk assessment related to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, or privacy to support the achievement of the entity's objectives.

## Policies

- **[Risk Management Standard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theopenlane/policy-hub/main/risk-management/risk-management.md)** - Establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC3.1.1** - The entity establishes organizational strategies and objectives that are used to determine entity structure and performance metrics.
- **CC3.1.2** - Key performance indicators (KPIs) of both the business performance and employee performance are developed in alignment with entity objectives and strategies.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Risk identification and management** - Risks to achievement of objectives are identified, analyzed, and managed through a formal risk assessment process.

## Example Evidence

- **Annual Strategic Objectives and KPI Review** - Organizational strategies, objectives, and KPIs used to determine entity structure and performance measurement
