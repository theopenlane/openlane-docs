# CC4.1

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"CC4.1"}
  name={"Conducts ongoing and/or separate evaluations"}
  category={"Security"}
  subcategory={"Monitoring Activities"}
  description={"The entity selects, develops, and performs ongoing and/or separate evaluations to ascertain whether the components of internal control are present and functioning. (COSO Principle 16)"}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Considers a Mix of Ongoing and Separate Evaluations</summary>

Management includes a balance of ongoing and separate evaluations.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Rate of Change</summary>

Management considers the rate of change in business and business processes when selecting and developing ongoing and separate evaluations.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Establishes Baseline Understanding</summary>

The design and current state of an internal control system are used to establish a baseline for ongoing and separate evaluations.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Uses Knowledgeable Personnel</summary>

Evaluators performing ongoing and separate evaluations have sufficient knowledge to understand what is being evaluated.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Integrates With Business Processes</summary>

Ongoing evaluations are built into the business processes and adjust to changing conditions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Adjusts Scope and Frequency</summary>

Management varies the scope and frequency of separate evaluations depending on risk.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Objectively Evaluates</summary>

Separate evaluations are performed periodically to provide objective feedback.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Different Types of Ongoing and Separate Evaluations</summary>

Management uses a variety of ongoing and separate risk and control evaluations to determine whether internal controls are present and functioning. Depending on the entity's objectives, such risk and control evaluations may include first- and second-line monitoring and control testing, internal audit assessments, compliance assessments, resilience assessments, vulnerability scans, security assessment, penetration testing, and third-party assessments.

</details>

## Policies

## Example Organization Controls

- **CC4.1.1** - The entity monitors key activities and alerts are generated when those activities are outside normal tolerances.
- **CC4.1.2** - Alerts are generated and sent to those responsible for taking action.
- **CC4.1.3** - A third-party independent System and Organization Controls 2 (SOC 2) audit is conducted annually.
- **CC4.1.4** - A third-party independent penetration test is conducted annually to identify and exploit vulnerabilities identified within the environment.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Control monitoring effectiveness** - Controls are monitored through ongoing evaluations and deficiencies are identified and communicated in a timely manner.

## Example Evidence

- **Penetration Test Report** - Most recent third-party penetration test report and remediation evidence

## Example Action Plans

- **Annual penetration test** - Annual penetration test by qualified third-party vendor to identify vulnerabilities in production systems. Required for SOC 2 compliance.
