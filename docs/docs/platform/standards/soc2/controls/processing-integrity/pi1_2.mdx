# PI1.2

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"PI1.2"}
  category={"Processing Integrity"}
  subcategory={"Additional Criteria for Processing Integrity"}
  description={"The entity implements policies and procedures over system inputs, including controls over completeness and accuracy, to result in products, services, and reporting to meet the entity’s objectives."}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Defines Characteristics of Processing Inputs</summary>

The characteristics of processing inputs that are necessary to meet requirements are defined.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evaluates Processing Inputs</summary>

Processing inputs are evaluated for compliance with defined input requirements.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Creates and Maintains Records of System Inputs</summary>

Records of system input activities are created and maintained completely and accurately in a timely manner.

</details>
