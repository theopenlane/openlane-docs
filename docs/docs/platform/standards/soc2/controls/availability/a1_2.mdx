# A1.2

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"A1.2"}
  category={"Availability"}
  subcategory={"Additional Criteria for Availability"}
  description={"The entity authorizes, designs, develops or acquires, implements, operates, approves, maintains, and monitors environmental protections, software, data backup processes, and recovery infrastructure to meet its objectives."}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Identifies Environmental Threats</summary>

As part of the risk assessment process, management identifies environmental threats that could impair the availability of the system, including threats resulting from adverse weather, failure of environmental control systems, electrical discharge, fire, and water.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Designs Detection Measures</summary>

Detection measures are implemented to identify anomalies that could result from environmental threat events.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Implements and Maintains Environmental Protection Mechanisms</summary>

Management implements and maintains environmental protection mechanisms to prevent and mitigate environmental events.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Implements Alerts to Analyze Anomalies</summary>

Management implements alerts that are communicated to personnel for analysis to identify environmental threat events.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Responds to Environmental Threat Events</summary>

Procedures are in place for responding to environmental threat events and for evaluating the effectiveness of those policies and procedures on a periodic basis. This includes automatic mitigation systems (for example, uninterruptible power system and generator back-up subsystem).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Communicates and Reviews Detected Environmental Threat Events</summary>

Detected environmental threat events are communicated to and reviewed by the individuals responsible for the management of the system, and actions are taken, if necessary.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Determines Data Requiring Backup</summary>

Data is evaluated to determine whether backup is required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Performs Data Backup</summary>

Procedures are in place for backing up data, monitoring to detect back-up failures, and initiating corrective action when such failures occur.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Addresses Offsite Storage</summary>

Back-up data is stored in a location at a distance from its principal storage location sufficient that the likelihood of a security or environmental threat event affecting both sets of data is reduced to an appropriate level.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Implements Alternate Processing Infrastructure</summary>

Measures are implemented for migrating processing to alternate infrastructure in the event normal processing infrastructure becomes unavailable. Measures may include geographic separation, redundancy, and failover capabilities for components.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Considers Data Recoverability</summary>

Management identifies threats to data recoverability (for example, ransomware attacks) that could impair the availability of the system and related data and implements mitigation procedures.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **A1.2.1** - GCP data centers have fire detection and suppression equipment in place.
- **A1.2.3** - GCP data centers have power management equipment in place.
- **A1.2.5** - GCP data centers have air conditioning and ventilation equipment to maintain required temperature and humidity levels.
- **A1.2.8** - Emergency procedures are in place to guide personnel in procedures to protect against disruptions caused by an unexpected event.
- **A1.2.9** - A business continuity plan is in place to guide personnel in the procedures to protect against disruption caused by an unexpected event.
- **A1.2.10** - Redundant architecture is in place to migrate business operations to alternate infrastructure in the event normal processing infrastructure becomes unavailable.
- **A1.2.11** - GCP regions are geographically separated to ensure recoverability in the event of an outage at the primary location.
- **A1.2.12** - Backup systems are in place to perform scheduled backups of Cloud SQL production databases at predefined times.
- **A1.2.13** - The backup systems are configured to send alert notifications to personnel regarding backup job status.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Environmental protection** - Environmental protections and redundancies are in place to prevent service disruption.

## Example Control Implementations

- **database-backup-recovery** - Database backups are automated with point-in-time recovery capability. Implementation includes: - Cloud SQL automated backups with 30-day retention - Point-in-time recovery enabled for production database - Backups stored in GCS with encryption - Annual DR test validates recovery procedures - recovery time objective (RTO): 4 hours, recovery point objective (RPO): 1 hour
