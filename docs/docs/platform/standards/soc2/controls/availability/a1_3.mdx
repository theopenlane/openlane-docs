## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Implements Business Continuity Plan Testing</summary>

Business continuity plan testing is performed on a periodic basis to test the entity's ability to respond, recover from, and resume operations through significant disruptions. Testing includes (1) development of testing scenarios based on threat likelihood and magnitude; (2) consideration of system components from across the entity and vendors that can impair availability; (3) scenarios that consider the potential for the lack of availability of key personnel or vendors; and (4) revision of continuity plans and systems based on test results.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Tests Integrity and Completeness of Back-Up Data</summary>

The integrity and completeness of back-up information is tested on a periodic basis.

</details>

## Example Organization Controls

- **A1.3.1** - A business continuity plan is documented and outlines the range of disaster scenarios and steps that the business will take to ensure the resumption of critical business operations.
- **A1.3.2** - Backup systems are in place to perform scheduled backups of production databases at predefined times.
- **A1.3.3** - Data backup restoration tests are performed at least annually.

## Example Control Objectives

- **Data recovery capability** - Data backup and recovery procedures ensure timely restoration of services.

## Example Control Implementations

- **database-backup-recovery** - Database backups are automated with point-in-time recovery capability. Implementation includes: - Cloud SQL automated backups with 30-day retention - Point-in-time recovery enabled for production database - Backups stored in GCS with encryption - Annual DR test validates recovery procedures - recovery time objective (RTO): 4 hours, recovery point objective (RPO): 1 hour

## Example Evidence

- **Backup Restoration Test Results** - Documentation of quarterly backup restoration test including time measurements and verification steps
- **Disaster Recovery Test Report** - Annual DR test documentation including scenario, results, and lessons learned

## Example Action Plans

- **Annual disaster recovery test** - Annual full disaster recovery test to validate RTO/RPO objectives and recovery procedures. Tests database restore, application deployment, and failover capabilities.

## Example Remediations

- **backup-verification-remediation** - Conduct and document quarterly backup restore tests to verify data recovery capabilities meet RTO/RPO objectives.
