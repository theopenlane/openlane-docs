# P8.1

<ControlMeta
  refCode={"P8.1"}
  category={"Privacy"}
  subcategory={"Privacy Criteria Related to Monitoring and Enforcement"}
  description={"The entity implements a process for receiving, addressing, resolving, and communicating the resolution of inquiries, complaints, and disputes from data subjects and others and periodically monitors compliance to meet the entity’s objectives related to privacy. Corrections and other necessary actions related to identified deficiencies are made or taken in a timely manner."}
/>

## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Communicates to Data Subjects or Data Controllers [P][C]</summary>

Data subjects or data controllers are informed about how to contact the entity with inquiries, complaints, and disputes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Addresses Inquiries, Complaints, and Disputes[P][C]</summary>

A process is in place to address inquiries, complaints, and disputes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Documents and Communicates Dispute Resolution and Recourse[P][C]</summary>

Each complaint is addressed, and the resolution is documented and communicated to the individual.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Documents and Reports Compliance Review Results[P][C]</summary>

Compliance with objectives related to privacy are reviewed and documented, and the results of such reviews are reported to management. If problems are identified, remediation plans are developed and implemented.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Documents and Reports Instances of Noncompliance[P][C]</summary>

Instances of noncompliance with objectives related to privacy are documented and reported and, if needed, corrective and disciplinary measures are taken on a timely basis.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Performs Ongoing Monitoring[P][C]</summary>

Ongoing procedures are performed for monitoring the effectiveness of controls over personal information and for taking timely corrective actions when necessary.

</details>
