## Points of Focus

<details>
<summary>Informs Data Subjects of Consequences of Failure to Provide Consent [C]</summary>

Data subjects are informed of the consequences of failing to provide the entity with explicit consent.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Documents Explicit Consent to Retain Information [C]</summary>

Documentation of explicit consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of sensitive personal information is retained to support the achievement of entity objectives related to privacy.

</details>
