# Registry

Registry is your compliance source of truth for who is involved, what you rely on, and what is in scope. Every registry record can be created individually, imported in bulk from CSV, or managed through the API and CLI; the examples on each page cover all of these.

Audits move faster when you can answer three questions quickly: who works in the environment, which third parties are involved, and which assets are in scope. Registry gives you those answers in one place.

## What's in This Section

- [Personnel](./personnel/overview.mdx) - Workforce records for employees and contractors (Identity Holders)
- [Entities](./entities.mdx) - Vendors and third parties your business depends on
- [Assets](./assets.mdx) - Systems, services, and devices you need to govern
- [Platforms](./platforms.mdx) - Systems and boundaries that define your compliance scope
- [Contacts](./contacts.mdx) - People behind your vendors and campaigns

## Typical Outcomes

- Cleaner audit scoping and faster evidence collection
- Better ownership and accountability across teams
- Stronger vendor and asset governance without spreadsheet churn
