# Compliance Management

Compliance Management holds the objects your program is built from, and they form a chain: [policies and procedures](./policy-and-procedure-management/policies.mdx) document what you intend to do, [controls](./controls/overview.mdx) operationalize those policies and map to the standards you're pursuing, [evidence](./evidence/overview.mdx) attaches to controls to prove they operate, and [programs](./programs/overview.mdx) bundle all of it for a given audit period. You can grant auditors access directly to Openlane instead of uploading evidence to third-party portals.

![How compliance objects relate in Openlane](/img/diagrams/object-model.svg)

If you are new to compliance concepts or preparing for your first audit, we suggest starting with [GRC Fundamentals](../grc-fundamentals/overview.mdx).
