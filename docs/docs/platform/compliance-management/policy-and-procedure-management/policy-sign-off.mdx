# Policy sign-off

Many organizations have a <Term t="controls">control</Term> stating that personnel acknowledge security policies when they join. To test that control, an auditor samples people and asks for each person's signed acknowledgment. An acknowledgment request sends one or more policies to recipients as PDFs, asks them to read each one and sign, and records which revision of each policy they signed.

## What recipients see

Openlane exports each policy you choose to a PDF and places it in a questionnaire, followed by your acknowledgment statement. Recipients scroll through every PDF to the end before the statement becomes available, so a signature always follows a full read of each document.

To sign, the recipient confirms the statement and accepts a typed signature generated from their name, or draws one instead. Openlane records the signing time, the recipient's email address, their IP address, and their browser with each signature. Recipients answer through a personal link in their email and do not need an Openlane account.

Each PDF carries the revision of the policy it was exported from, and the questionnaire stays linked to the policy. When you publish a new revision, existing signatures remain tied to the revision the recipient read. To collect sign-off on the new revision, send a new request.

## Send an acknowledgment request

You can start a request from the `...` menu on the **Policies** table, from the actions menu on the policies report, or from the `...` menu on a policy's page. Starting from a policy's page selects that policy for you.

<Steps>

1. Select **Send acknowledgment request**.
1. Choose the policies to include. One request can cover up to 10 policies, and recipients sign once for all of them.
1. Review the name and the acknowledgment statement. The name defaults to the policy name, and the statement defaults to "I acknowledge that I’ve read and understand the documents above." Edit either one to match your own wording.
1. Select **Save draft** to create the questionnaire without sending it, or select **Send now** to continue.
1. Add recipients by choosing personnel or entering email addresses, and set a due date if you need one. Select **Preview** to open the questionnaire as recipients will see it.
1. Select **Create & send**.

</Steps>

Each recipient receives an email with their personal link, and the confirmation in the console links to the questionnaire. If any send fails, the request is still created, and you can send to those recipients again from the questionnaire.

A saved draft appears with your other questionnaires under [**Questionnaires**](https://console.theopenlane.io/automation/questionnaires), where you can edit it and send it later.

:::info[Important]
In this flow you select each recipient individually. To send to a larger group or on a schedule, use a [campaign](#send-through-a-campaign).
:::

## Track responses

The **Acknowledgments** tab on each policy lists every request that includes the policy. Expand a request to see each recipient and where their response stands. You can also export the responses from the questionnaire to hand to an auditor.

Responses also appear on each person's record in [Personnel](../../registry/personnel/overview.mdx), so you can review everything one person has signed without opening each policy.

## Customize the questionnaire

An acknowledgment request is a standard questionnaire, so you can change it in the questionnaire editor after you save it as a draft. Reword the statement, add questions before the signature, or attach more documents. A PDF question can attach a policy (which records its revision), an uploaded PDF, or a PDF linked by URL. Signature questions are available in any questionnaire, not only in acknowledgment requests.

To reuse the same layout for later requests, open the questionnaire and select **Save as Template**. [Building a questionnaire](../../automation/assessments.mdx#building-a-questionnaire) covers templates and the editor. The email recipients receive uses your campaign email template and branding, described in [email delivery and branding](../../automation/campaigns.mdx#email-delivery-and-branding).

## Send through a campaign

Acknowledgment requests send directly and do not create a [campaign](../../automation/campaigns.mdx). We recommend a campaign when the same sign-off repeats on a schedule, such as an annual acceptable use acknowledgment, because a campaign reruns on its own schedule and resends only to the people who have not finished.

<Steps>

1. Follow the first three steps of [Send an acknowledgment request](#send-an-acknowledgment-request), then select **Save draft**.
1. Open the saved questionnaire under [**Questionnaires**](https://console.theopenlane.io/automation/questionnaires) and select **Save as Template**.
1. Create a campaign, select the template you saved, and add your recipients and schedule.

</Steps>

Campaign responses are tracked on the campaign and on each person's record. The [Questionnaires](../../automation/assessments.mdx) page covers how recipients respond and how follow-ups work.

## Audience-based sign-off <StatusChip status="coming-soon" />

Acknowledgment requests will send through campaigns directly, with audiences defined by rule instead of by name. For example, you will be able to send a policy to all personnel of type employee on a set interval. Set the audience and cadence once, and new personnel who match the rule are included in the next run.

Until then, use the [campaign steps](#send-through-a-campaign) above for recurring sign-off.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do recipients need an Openlane account to sign?</summary>

No. Each recipient answers through a personal link sent to their email address.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to signatures when a policy changes?</summary>

They stay tied to the revision the recipient read. A new revision needs a new acknowledgment request, which gives you a separate record of who signed each version.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a recipient sign without reading the policy?</summary>

No. The acknowledgment statement and signature stay unavailable until the recipient has scrolled to the end of every attached PDF.

</details>
