# Risk Management Frameworks (RMF)

A risk management framework describes the vocabulary, tools, and techniques for a coherent approach to risk, so all stakeholders work from the same definitions. The frameworks below are the ones you are most likely to encounter; each links to its source material so you can pick one to lean on.

Enterprise frameworks address _any type of risk_ that could prevent the company from achieving its business objectives, while others focus on information security, cybersecurity, and privacy protection.

## COSO Enterprise Risk Management

The Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO) helps organizations improve <Term t="internal-control">internal control</Term> and manage enterprise-wide risk.

<StandardMeta
  fullName="COSO Enterprise Risk Management Framework"
  type="Enterprise risk framework"
  managedBy="Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO)"
  appliesTo="Any type of organizational risk"
  source="[ERM Framework (2020)](https://www.coso.org/Documents/Compliance-Risk-Management-Applying-the-COSO-ERM-Framework.pdf)"
/>

## Factor Analysis of Information Risk (FAIR)

FAIR quantifies risk in financial terms rather than qualitative ratings, which makes it the common choice when leadership wants risk expressed in dollars.

<StandardMeta
  fullName="Factor Analysis of Information Risk"
  type="Quantitative risk model"
  managedBy="FAIR Institute"
  appliesTo="Information security and operational risk"
  source="[fairinstitute.org](https://www.fairinstitute.org/)"
/>

## International Organization for Standardization (ISO)

ISO publishes two complementary risk standards: ISO/IEC 27005 for information security risks specifically, and ISO 31000 for risk of any type.

<StandardMeta
  fullName="ISO/IEC 27005:2022, Guidance on managing information security risks"
  type="International standard"
  managedBy="[ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27](https://www.iso.org/committee/45306.html)"
  appliesTo="Information security risks; applies to an ISMS as specified in ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002"
  source="[iso.org](https://www.iso.org/standard/80585.html)"
/>

<StandardMeta
  fullName="ISO 31000, Risk management guidelines"
  type="International standard (guidelines)"
  managedBy="[ISO/TC 262](https://www.iso.org/committee/629121.html)"
  appliesTo="Any type of risk, customizable to any organization and context"
  source="[iso.org](https://www.iso.org/iso-31000-risk-management.html)"
/>

The ISO 31000 umbrella includes several related specifications, some still under development:

* [ISO 31000 Risk management - Guidelines](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html)
* [ISO 31000:2019 Risk Management - Risk Assessment Techniques](https://www.iso.org/standard/72140.html)
* [ISO 31022:2020 Risk Management - Guidelines for the management of legal risk](https://www.iso.org/standard/69295.html)
* [ISO/FDIS 31030 Travel Risk Management - Guidance for organizations](https://www.iso.org/standard/54204.html)
* [ISO/AWI 31050 - Guidance for managing emerging risks to enhance resilience](https://www.iso.org/standard/54224.html)
* [ISO/DIS 31073 Risk Management - Vocabulary](https://www.iso.org/standard/79637.html)

## NIST Risk Management Framework

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) RMF integrates security, privacy, and risk management activities into the system development life cycle, and is the framework behind Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA) compliance for US federal systems.

<StandardMeta
  fullName="NIST Risk Management Framework"
  type="Federal framework"
  managedBy="National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)"
  appliesTo="US federal information systems under [FISMA](https://www.cisa.gov/federal-information-security-modernization-act), and voluntary adopters"
  source="[csrc.nist.gov](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/risk-management)"
/>

Note that [NIST SP 800-53 revision 5](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-53r5.pdf) describes the Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations and [SP 800-53B](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-53B.pdf) describes the control baselines. Related publications:

* [SP 800-37: Risk Management Framework for Information Systems and Organizations](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-37/rev-2/final)
* [SP 800-39: Managing Information Security Risk](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-39/final)
* [SP 800-30 Rev. 1: Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-30/rev-1/final)
* [SP 800-53A: Assessing Security and Privacy Controls](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53a/rev-4/final)
* [SP 800-161: Supply Chain Risk Management Practices](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-161/final)
