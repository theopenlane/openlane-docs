# Branding

Customize your Trust Center to match your organization's brand. You can configure your company information, logo, colors, and theme.

## Preview Before Publishing

Branding changes aren't immediately public. When you make changes:

<Steps>

1. They're saved to a preview state
1. Use the preview URL to see how they look
1. Click **Publish** to make them live
1. Use **Revert Changes** to discard unpublished changes

</Steps>

The preview URL is shown at the top of the branding page.

## Security Contact Email

The security email address appears on your Trust Center as the contact for responsible disclosure. We recommend an address that routes to your security team (typically something like `security@yourcompany.com`) so reports reach the people who can act on them.

Security researchers and customers use this to report potential vulnerabilities.

## Theme Options

You can choose between:

- **Easy mode**: Pick a primary color and the system derives the rest
- **Advanced mode**: Full control over the font and individual colors (foreground, background, accent, secondary foreground, and secondary background)

### Detect Branding from Domain

Instead of setting colors by hand, click **Detect Branding from Domain** in the Theme section and Openlane will read the branding from your company website:

- It picks up your colors, fonts, logo, and favicon
- Results go into your preview settings only; nothing goes live until you publish
- Assets you've already uploaded stay unchanged
- Any unsaved edits on the branding page are replaced by what's pulled

Choose the domain to scan from the list. It's built from the domains in [Organization Settings](https://console.theopenlane.io/organization-settings/general-settings) and the company domain on the branding page; use **Manage Domains** to add or change them. The scan can take a few minutes. You can navigate away, and you'll get a notification when it's done.

### Live Color Preview

In Advanced mode, click **Generate palette** to open your preview site with a color generator panel. Change any of the five colors and the page updates as you go, so you can see the result on your actual Trust Center instead of switching back and forth. **Generate dark** and **Generate light** create a starting palette for you, and **Reset to saved colors** returns to what's saved.

When you're happy with the colors:

<Steps>

1. Click **Copy** in the color generator. It copies a palette string such as `olbrand1:bg=#ffffff;fg=#09151d;accent=#2dcdad;secBg=#ffffff;secFg=#09151d`
1. Back on the branding page, paste it into **Import palette** and click **Apply**
1. Click **Save Preview** to check it on your preview site, then **Publish** to make it live

</Steps>

The color generator is only available on your preview site, so visitors to your live Trust Center never see it.

## Logo and Favicon

Upload your logo for the Trust Center header and a favicon for browser tabs.

- Supported formats: PNG, JPG, SVG
- Maximum file size: 5MB
- Transparent backgrounds work best for logos

You can upload files directly or provide URLs to hosted images.

## Title and Overview

The title and overview text appear prominently on your Trust Center and are used for SEO. Write an overview that communicates your organization's commitment to security, privacy, and transparency.

Example:
> At [Company], security and privacy are core to how we build our platform. This Trust Center provides an overview of our security practices and access to relevant documentation.

## Best Practices

- Use a high-contrast color scheme for accessibility
- Ensure your logo renders well on both light and dark backgrounds
- Write a clear overview that communicates your security commitment
- Always include a valid security contact email
