# FAQs

The FAQs section of your Trust Center holds questions and answers that visitors can read without contacting you. Use it for the security questions prospects ask during procurement or a security review, so the answer is already published when they go looking for it.

## How FAQs appear

FAQs show up in a **Frequently Asked Questions** section on your Trust Center, and each question expands to show its answer. When an FAQ has a reference link, a **Learn more** link appears under the answer. The section and its entry in the Trust Center navigation appear once you have at least one FAQ.

FAQs can be grouped by [category](#categories).

## Adding FAQs

From the [FAQs page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/faqs):

<Steps>

1. Enter the **Question** and **Answer**. Both are required.
1. Add a **Reference Link** to a page where buyers can read more, such as a security whitepaper or policy page, if you have one.
1. Choose a **Category** or create a new one, if you group your FAQs.
1. Click **Publish FAQ**.

</Steps>

An FAQ goes live as soon as you add it, without going through the branding preview, and new FAQs join the end of the list.

To bring over FAQs from another trust center or a spreadsheet, click **Bulk Upload** on the FAQs page. See [Migrating from another trust center](./migrating.mdx#faqs).

## Ordering, editing, and deleting

Drag an FAQ by its handle to set the order it appears in on your Trust Center. Click the edit icon to change its question, answer, reference link, or category, or click the delete icon and confirm to remove it. A deleted FAQ cannot be restored.

## Categories

Categories help buyers find the answer they need in a longer list, for example **Security**, **Privacy**, or **Infrastructure**. FAQ categories are a [custom enum](../settings/custom-data.mdx), so you manage the list of values the same way as other custom enums. You can also create a category while adding or editing an FAQ if you have permission to create custom enums. Otherwise, choose from the existing ones.

If no FAQ has a category, your Trust Center shows them as a single list. Once any FAQ has a category, FAQs are grouped under a heading for each category, and any FAQ without one is grouped under **Other**.

## Best practices

- Start with the questions your sales and security teams answer most often in questionnaires
- Keep answers short and link to supporting documents for detail rather than pasting whole policies
- Point reference links at public pages, since [private documents](./documents.mdx) still require visitors to request access
- Review answers when your practices change so the Trust Center does not contradict your latest audit or policies
