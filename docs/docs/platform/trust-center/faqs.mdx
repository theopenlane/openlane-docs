# FAQs

Answer the security questions buyers ask most, right on your Trust Center. Prospects working through procurement or a security review often need the same handful of answers (how data is encrypted, where it's hosted, how access is controlled), and publishing them up front saves both sides a round of email.

## How FAQs Appear

FAQs show up in a **Frequently Asked Questions** section on your Trust Center, with each question expanding to show its answer. If an FAQ has a reference link, a **Learn more** link appears under the answer. The section, and its entry in the Trust Center navigation, only appear once you have at least one FAQ.

FAQs can be grouped by [category](#categories).

## Adding FAQs

From the [FAQs page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/faqs):

<Steps>

1. Enter the **Question** and **Answer**. Both are required.
1. Optionally add a **Reference Link** to a page where buyers can read more, such as a security whitepaper or policy page
1. Optionally choose a **Category**, or create a new one
1. Click **Publish FAQ**

</Steps>

FAQs are published as soon as you add them; they don't go through the branding preview. New FAQs are added to the end of the list.

To bring over FAQs from another trust center or a spreadsheet, click **Bulk Upload** on the FAQs page. See [Migrating from Another Trust Center](./migrating.mdx#faqs).

## Ordering, Editing, and Deleting

- **Reorder**: drag FAQs by their handle to set the order they appear in on your Trust Center
- **Edit**: click the edit icon on an FAQ to change its question, answer, reference link, or category
- **Delete**: click the delete icon and confirm. Deleting an FAQ can't be undone.

## Categories

Categories help buyers find the answer they need in a longer list, for example **Security**, **Privacy**, or **Infrastructure**. FAQ categories are a [custom enum](../settings/custom-data.mdx), so you manage the list of values the same way as other custom enums. You can also create a new category while adding or editing an FAQ if you have permission to create custom enums; otherwise choose from the existing ones.

How categories affect your Trust Center:

- **No categories set**: FAQs are shown as a single list, without grouping
- **Some categories set**: FAQs are grouped under a heading for each category, and any FAQ without a category is grouped under **Other**

## Best Practices

- Start with the questions your sales and security teams answer most often in questionnaires
- Keep answers short and link to supporting documents for detail rather than pasting whole policies
- Point reference links at public pages; [private documents](./documents.mdx) still require visitors to request access
- Review answers when your practices change so the Trust Center doesn't contradict your latest audit or policies
