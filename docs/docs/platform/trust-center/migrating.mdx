# Migrating from another trust center

If you already run a trust center elsewhere, you can bring its lists into Openlane without re-entering them. Export each list from your current tool as a CSV, then import it with the same [upload, map, and review flow](../importing-data.mdx#how-import-works) used everywhere in Openlane. Your column names do not need to match ours, because you map them during import.

| What | Import | Required columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Subprocessors](./subprocessors.mdx) | [Import subprocessors](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcentersubprocessor) | `Name` |
| [FAQs](./faqs.mdx) | [Import FAQs](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcenterfaq) | `Question`, `Answer` |
| [Signed NDAs](./ndas.mdx) | [Import signed NDAs](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcenterndarequest) | `Email`, `First Name`, `Last Name` |
| [Subscribers](./notifications.mdx) | `Bulk Upload` on the [Subscribers page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/subscribers) | `Email` |

You set up your [custom domain](./custom-domain.mdx), [documents](./documents.mdx), [frameworks](./frameworks.mdx), and [customer logos](./customer-logos.mdx) directly in Openlane rather than importing them. Your branding does not need rebuilding by hand either. [Detect it from your website](./branding.mdx#detect-branding-from-domain) instead.

## Recommended order

<Steps>

1. **Set up your branding.** Click **Detect Branding from Domain** on the [branding page](./branding.mdx#detect-branding-from-domain) to pull your colors, fonts, logo, and favicon from your website into preview. Fine-tune the colors with the [live color preview](./branding.mdx#live-color-preview) and publish when it matches.
1. **Upload your NDA document.** Signed NDAs can be imported only after your NDA is on the [NDAs page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/NDAs).
1. **[Import subprocessors](#subprocessors).**
1. **[Import FAQs](#faqs).**
1. **[Import signed NDAs](#signed-ndas).**
1. **[Import documents](documents).**
1. **[Select frameworks](frameworks).**
1. **[Select implemented controls](controls).**
1. **[Highlight your customers](customer-logos).**
1. **Point your [custom domain](./custom-domain.mdx) at Openlane.** Do this once everything is in place, so visitors switch over to a complete Trust Center. Your default Openlane URL is [hidden from search engines](./overview.mdx#search-engine-visibility) from the start, so the Trust Center stays out of search results while you migrate. Leave that setting on so results point to your own domain.
1. **[Import subscribers](#subscribers).** Do this last, so your subscribers start with fresh updates instead of old content.

</Steps>

## Subprocessors

Open [Import subprocessors](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcentersubprocessor), or select `Bulk Upload` from the `...` menu on the [Subprocessors page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/subprocessors). `Name` is the only required column. You can also map:

| Column | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Fetches a logo for subprocessors created as custom ones |
| Description | Describes what the subprocessor does |
| Countries | Lists where the subprocessor operates, as country names or ISO codes separated by `;`, `\|`, or `,` |
| Category | Sets the category shown in your Trust Center, creating new categories as needed |
| Tags and Logo URL | Apply to subprocessors created as custom ones |

```csv
Subprocessor,Website,Purpose,Location,Category
Amazon Web Services,aws.amazon.com,Cloud infrastructure hosting,United States; Germany,Infrastructure
Acme Analytics,acme-analytics.io,Product usage analytics,US,Analytics
```

Subprocessor imports have a **Match subprocessors** step instead of **Review**. Openlane matches each name in your file against its subprocessor catalog and your own custom subprocessors, so well-known vendors such as AWS link to the existing catalog entry with its logo and details.

A row that matches links to the existing subprocessor. Matches on a close spelling or an acronym are marked as suggestions, which you can review one by one or accept together with `Confirm suggestions`. A row that matches nothing becomes a custom subprocessor with a logo from its website, and its details let you add or correct the website, logo, description, or tags. You can point any row at a different subprocessor, create it as a custom subprocessor, or choose `Don't import this row`.

Set default countries and a default category to fill in rows that have no value in your file. Values that are not countries, such as a region name, are listed so you can choose the countries each one stands for, and every row using that value updates. Rows already in your Trust Center and duplicates within the file are flagged so they are not added twice.

Use the tabs above the list to work through long files, then click `Add` to add the subprocessors to your Trust Center.

## FAQs

Open [Import FAQs](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcenterfaq), or click `Bulk Upload` on the [FAQs page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/faqs). `Question` and `Answer` are required. You can also map a `Reference Link` where buyers can read more and a `Category`, and Openlane creates any category that does not exist yet.

```csv
Question,Answer,Category,Reference Link
Do you encrypt customer data at rest?,Yes. All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256.,Security,https://example.com/security
```

On the **Review** step, drag the rows to set the order of the imported FAQs. They always land after any FAQs you already have, so reordering here changes only their order relative to each other.

## Signed NDAs

If visitors already signed your NDA in your previous trust center, import those records so your NDA history carries over. Open [Import signed NDAs](https://console.theopenlane.io/import?type=trustcenterndarequest), or click `Import Signed NDAs` on the [NDAs page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/NDAs).

`Email`, `First Name`, and `Last Name` are required. You can also map the signer's company and the date they signed, and columns such as `Organization`, `Employer`, `Date Signed`, or `Executed On` are recognized for you.

```csv
Email,First Name,Last Name,Company,Date Signed
jordan@customer.com,Jordan,Lee,Customer Inc,2026-03-14
```

Each imported record counts as signed and links to your Trust Center and your current NDA document.

## Subscribers

On the [Subscribers page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/subscribers), select `Bulk Upload` from the `...` menu. `Email` is the only required column. Your Trust Center must be [accepting new subscribers](./notifications.mdx#accepting-subscribers), so turn on `Allow new subscribers` first if it is off.

Imported subscribers are marked as verified and start receiving [notifications](./notifications.mdx) right away.

:::warning
By uploading subscribers, you confirm that you have permission to add and contact these recipients. Imported subscribers will not be asked to verify their email address.
:::
