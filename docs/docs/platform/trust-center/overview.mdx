# Trust Center

The Trust Center is a public portal intended to be viewed by your customers and prospects, where you share your security posture, compliance certifications, and data protection practices. Instead of responding to <Term t="security-questionnaires">security questionnaires</Term> one by one, you give customers and prospects a single place to find the information they need.

Every sales cycle involves security questions: prospects want your System and Organization Controls 2 (SOC 2) report, your certifications, and to know who processes their data. Publishing the answers once, publicly or behind an NDA, lets prospects self-serve what your security team would otherwise handle request by request, and the <Term t="subprocessor">subprocessor</Term> list satisfies the disclosure obligations regulations like General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) impose.

## Migrating from Another Trust Center

Already running a trust center somewhere else? You don't have to rebuild it by hand. Pull your branding from your website, then import your subprocessors, FAQs, signed NDAs, and subscribers from CSV exports of your current tool. See [Migrating from Another Trust Center](./migrating.mdx) for the recommended order.

## What You Can Share

### Documents

Upload security documentation like SOC 2 reports, International Organization for Standardization (ISO) certifications, penetration test summaries, and security policies. Control who can access each document:

- **Public**: Anyone can download
- **Private**: Requires signing an NDA first

### Frameworks

Display the compliance certifications you've achieved (SOC 2, ISO 27001, Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), etc.). Only enable frameworks you've actually completed; this is a trust signal to customers.

### Subprocessors

List the third-party services that process customer data on your behalf. This transparency helps customers with their own compliance and vendor management.

### FAQs

Answer the security questions buyers ask most, grouped by category, so prospects get answers without waiting on a questionnaire. See [FAQs](./faqs.mdx).

### Updates

Share brief announcements about new certifications, policy changes, or security improvements. This shows active security management.

## Preview and Publish

Changes you make aren't immediately public. The Trust Center uses a draft/publish workflow:

<Steps>

1. Make changes in the console
2. Preview them using the preview URL
3. Publish when you're ready to go live

</Steps>

This lets you review changes before customers see them.

## Your Trust Center URLs

- **Default URL**: `https://trust.theopenlane.net/{your-organization-slug}`
- **Vanity Domain**: Your custom domain (e.g., `https://trust.yourcompany.com`) if configured

Both URLs work, even after setting up a <Term t="vanity-domain">vanity domain</Term>.

### Search Engine Visibility

Your preview site is always hidden from search engines, so drafts never show up in Google results.

Your default URL is also hidden from search engines when your Trust Center is created. When you're ready for it to appear in search results, turn off **Hide from search engines** on the [domain settings page](https://console.theopenlane.io/trust-center/domain). If you use a vanity domain, you'll usually leave it on so search results point to your own domain instead of the Openlane URL.

The setting only affects the default URL; your vanity domain is never hidden by it.
