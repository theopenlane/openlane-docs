# Automation

Automation keeps recurring compliance work running without manual coordination. We give you a small set of tools, each suited to a different shape of work:

- [**Workflows**](./workflows/overview.mdx): gate object changes behind reviews. Define triggers and conditions, and the engine stages changes, routes approvals, and tracks the decision trail.
- [**Workflow Definitions**](./workflows/workflow-definitions.mdx): the authoring reference for writing definitions, covering triggers, Common Expression Language (CEL) expressions, actions, and copyable examples.
- [**Campaigns**](./campaigns.mdx): collect something from many people at once, such as policy attestations, training acknowledgements, and vendor questionnaires. Recipient-level tracking and follow-up resends are built in.
- [**Assessments**](./assessments.mdx): the reusable questionnaire content campaigns send. Maintain one definition; run it as many times as you need.
- [**Tasks**](./tasks.mdx): individual owned work with a deadline.

None of these depends on the others to be useful; you can run campaigns without ever defining a workflow, or use tasks alone. They compose where it helps: a campaign distributes an assessment and tracks every recipient's response; an approval workflow assigns its reviews as work items.

## Picking the Right Tool

| You need... | Reach for |
| --- | --- |
| Policy changes reviewed and approved before they take effect | A [workflow](./workflows/overview.mdx) with pre-commit approval |
| Every employee to attest to the acceptable use policy annually | A [campaign](./campaigns.mdx) targeting personnel |
| 40 vendors to complete a security questionnaire | A [campaign](./campaigns.mdx) sending an [assessment](./assessments.mdx) |
| One person to upload specific evidence by Friday | A [task](./tasks.mdx) |

Recurring governance work (access reviews, attestations, <Term t="evidence">evidence</Term> refresh) is the easiest part of a program to fall behind on. Running it through automation standardizes the cadence, reduces manual handoffs and missed deadlines, and leaves a traceable record for audits and customer assurance: who was asked, who responded, who approved, and when.
